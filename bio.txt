Dimitrios S. Nikolopoulos is the John W. Hancock Professor of Engineering in the Department of Computer Science at Virginia Tech. His research covers the programming and orchestration of multiprocessors and accelerators for high-performance computing, cloud computing, and artificial intelligence. His current work focuses on efficient and resilient AI systems. He previously headed the School of Electronics, Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at Queen's University Belfast. He is a Fellow of the IEEE, a Royal Society Wolfson Fellow, and a Distinguished Member of the ACM. He received his Ph.D. from the University of Patras, Greece.
