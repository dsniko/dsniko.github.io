Dimitrios S. Nikolopoulos is the John W. Hancock Jr. Professor of Engineering in the Department of Computer Science at Virginia Tech. He holds a courtesy appointment in Electrical and Computer Engineering. His research covers programming and orchestration of multiprocessors and accelerators for high-performance computing, cloud computing, and AI. He is a Fellow of the IEEE, IET, and BCS, and a Distinguished Member of the ACM. He received a Royal Society Wolfson Research Merit Award. He has won best paper awards at SC, PPoPP, IPDPS, and DATE. He chairs the ICS Steering Committee. He headed the School of EEECS at Queen’s University Belfast.
