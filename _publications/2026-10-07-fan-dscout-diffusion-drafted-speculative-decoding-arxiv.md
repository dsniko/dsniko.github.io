excerpt: "DScout uses two factorizations to make diffusion drafters faster in speculative decoding. A loss factorization guides training toward more accepted tokens. A throughput factorization picks the conditioning depth, drafter depth, and block size for each workload. Across six benchmarks, DScout reaches mean speedups of 3.84x on Qwen3-4B and 4.14x on Qwen3-8B over autoregressive decoding."
