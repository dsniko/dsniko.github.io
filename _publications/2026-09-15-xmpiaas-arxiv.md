excerpt: "XMPIaaS relocates MPI process groups between cloud nodes without restarting the job. A cooperative protocol between the Hydra process manager and the rank processes quiesces only the affected ranks, then checkpoints and restores them with CRIU. The quiesce phase accounts for under 1.4% of migration downtime, and downtime tracks the migrating node's rank count rather than job size."
