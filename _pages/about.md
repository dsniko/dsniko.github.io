I am the John W. Hancock Jr. Professor of Engineering at Virginia Tech. My home department is [Computer Science](https://www.cs.vt.edu), and I hold a courtesy appointment in [Electrical and Computer Engineering](https://ece.vt.edu/). I direct the [PEARL](https://pearl-lab-vt.github.io) laboratory and serve as associate director of the [Stack@CS Center for Computer Systems](https://stack.cs.vt.edu).

My research concerns how multiprocessors and accelerators are programmed and orchestrated. This work spans high-performance computing, cloud computing, and artificial intelligence. It has informed the [OpenMP](https://www.openmp.org) standard and is used in Red Hat Enterprise Linux and commercial compilers.

My current work focuses on efficient and resilient AI systems across the computing continuum. I also study performance engineering for modern accelerators and systems for data-intensive science. A common thread runs through all three: reducing the infrastructure footprint of AI.

I am a Fellow of the IEEE, a Royal Society Wolfson Fellow, and a Distinguished Member of the ACM. I chair the Steering Committee of the [ACM International Conference on Supercomputing](https://www.ics-conference.org/). Before returning to Virginia Tech, I was Head of the School of Electronics, Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at Queen's University Belfast. I received my Ph.D. from the University of Patras in 2000.
